# openpocket

## 1.0.0-beta.1

### Major Changes

- [#58](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/58) [`6c92e81`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/6c92e8193262da2ecaee0a051dcabb110aade14e) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The guitar firmware is now Gamut 1.0b1 (`PocketX_950`, `BlackBox_950`), formerly OpenPocket v43. Its boot screen shows Gamut, its editor and the installer call it Gamut with the version 1.0b1, and the site, the README and the documents use the new name. The sound and the controls are those of v43. The USB device name, the update loader's identity and the package names stay as they were, so this installer, the editor and the original OpenPocket project's installer all still find the pedal; that installer names it OpenPocket v50.

- [#48](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/48) [`013175b`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/013175b5ae44190f4d2f878821d866baafe23f51) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The built-in amps and cabinets are removed: the AMP block plays NAM models and the CAB block plays your own IRs, and a preset saved with a built-in amp now plays the NAM model of the same amp, while one saved with a built-in cabinet plays the IR in slot 1 (DI turns the cabinet off).

- [#51](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/51) [`85d4bb8`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/85d4bb88cddcc8e99f422a9fbb3b86cb99bcdd76) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - OpenPocket is now v43 (`PocketX_943`, `BlackBox_943`), so a pedal running it can be told apart from one running v42. Since v42, the built-in amps and cabinets are gone, the preset store is format 6 and converts a preset saved by an earlier version when it loads, ML Sound Lab's "BEST IR IN THE WORLD" in slot IR1 is the only built-in cabinet IR, and the editor protocol is version 5.

### Minor Changes

- [#66](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/66) [`3002056`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/3002056482e7cb5faa152dfa2941cbfa78c57ae1) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The web editor connects to an M-VAVE Chocolate or Chocolate Plus over Bluetooth (in Chrome and Edge) and makes it drive the pedal: in the MIDI section it knows which model is connected and shows its controls, each footswitch is taught by pressing it, and you choose what it does: the next or the previous preset, one preset, an effect on or off, tap tempo for the delay, or a setting for the expression pedal. It works with the normal firmware while the editor is open, the mapping is kept in the browser for each model, and Undo takes a change back.

- [#50](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/50) [`8a41040`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/8a4104068924a7ea4ec5bec44c082aaeec86e77b) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - Slot IR1 now comes with a cabinet IR, "BEST IR IN THE WORLD" by ML Sound Lab ([video](https://www.facebook.com/watch/?ref=embed_video&v=1053603041469100)), redistributed with the creator's permission as stated by the project's owner: the factory presets play it until you load your own IR into IR1, and removing yours brings it back. The factory presets' levels are set for it.

- [#96](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/96) [`6dfb8be`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/6dfb8be690f9637f6b61b4cd3c8c6b6e51d36528) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - Gamut gets ready for 48 kHz. A cabinet IR is now loaded at the pedal's own sample rate, and the pedal plays an IR made at the other rate resampled, so your saved IRs keep their sound when Gamut moves to 48 kHz; the editor's note on a NAM capture's sample rate now compares it with the pedal's. At 44.1 kHz the sound is unchanged. A firmware that runs at 48 kHz offers USB audio at 48 kHz only, and the simulated pedal's sound runs at 48 kHz.

- [#69](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/69) [`d898d24`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/d898d24a66c05caf68770a2b5075181a1c35af8b) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The pedal's screen has new type: B612 for words and B612 Mono for figures, with labels, hints and settings in small letters (`gain`, `knob: block`, `363 ms`). The factory presets' names are in small letters too (`twin clean`, `user 25`); a sound you saved keeps the name it was saved with, and the names of models and IRs are shown as they are stored. The preset number and the tuner's note keep their old type for now.

- [#75](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/75) [`24eae2f`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/24eae2f5aa101e0fb8cd647a60eacda18678ed38) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The pedal's play screen has a new layout. The preset number is larger (58 px figures) and the label of the selected block stands beside it in large letters, pale while the block is on and grey while it is off. The chain is two rows of keys along a line: a block that is on is a pale key on the line, a block that is off a dark key below it, and a bar under a row marks the selected block; a block picked up to be moved rises above the line in amber. The amp's and the cabinet's names are no longer on the play screen: a block's model is on its page, one PUSH away. The tuner's note is larger (81 px) over a scale with a needle, the footers name each control and what it does (`knob block  push edit`), an overload is a taller amber band with a warning sign, and the update screen shows a dot for each step.

- [#65](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/65) [`46064bd`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/46064bdfe59f966263f17cb019634d8ee66c1169) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The pedal's screen has a new palette: a dark ground with pale text, on which a block that is on is a pale key and a block that is off is a dark key. Green is now a lamp and nothing else: it marks a block that is on, the playing preset in the list, a connected computer and a note in tune. Amber is the only warning colour, for an unsaved edit, a model that is loading, an overload, a late block, a low battery and a fault; nothing is drawn in red. The blocks no longer have colours of their own on the pedal, and the web editor keeps them.

- [#77](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/77) [`7b24199`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/7b241999f356f9f7a5d1abb6aedb562002e4763d) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The pedal's start, crash and rescue screens are in Gamut's design. A start shows the mark, the name `gamut` in small letters and the version. The report of a start that failed is an amber band with a warning sign, what to do, and the figures for a bug report in two columns; the start after it says `safe mode` in amber. A crash is graphite on amber instead of white on red, and the USB rescue mode shows the mark with its lamp unlit. Nothing on the pedal's screen is red any more.

- [#72](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/72) [`defe18d`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/defe18df26536874477a637a48ee50c6590ec290) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - Three MIDI Control Change numbers for a controller's footswitches: CC 116 taps the delay's tempo (two taps or more set the delay time), and CC 118 and 119 step to the next and the previous preset, as the pedal's footswitches do. Each acts at value 64 and up.

- [#71](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/71) [`c01bed2`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/c01bed211a8fd055a36d3c55ec07b1b08761308a) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The web editor adds NAM captures in browsers that open no second window or script-opened file picker, such as MIDIWeb on an iPhone: TONE3000 signs in and lets you choose a tone in the editor's own tab and comes back with the captures, and the file buttons are real file inputs.

- [#78](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/78) [`3d2078f`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/3d2078f818b6bebd06cab5ea6d1b86f7bf669cce) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - Gamut's MIDI page has a USB PORT row: FOOTSWITCH makes the pedal the USB MIDI host of a footswitch plugged into its USB-C port, such as an M-VAVE Chocolate through a powered OTG adapter, and every power-on starts as the normal USB device again. After ettorebox by Roberto Antonacci.

### Patch Changes

- [#67](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/67) [`a356e06`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/a356e06d32c52f44e23389fa0929bcb601b095e8) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The Bluetooth test image (`make ble`) builds again: its Bluetooth library is trimmed at build time, so the image fits its flash area. The normal firmware is unchanged.

- [#76](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/76) [`bb223de`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/bb223de0dbded65db2c3187270fbf522e33c4b38) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The Bluetooth test image's timers no longer stall for three minutes each time the pedal's clock wraps, can be set from the radio's interrupts safely, and the radio's short scheduling timeouts now run on time, which should keep a Bluetooth MIDI link from dropping.

- [#74](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/74) [`4f9b968`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/4f9b968e24e2900127cbfcaf438e025cd67c1d26) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - When the second processor faults and is switched off, the pedal now keeps its registers and stack pointer, and the Bluetooth test image's log (`node tools/op.mjs log`) shows them, so the cause can be found. The processor is still not started again.

- [#37](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/37) [`de83bb9`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/de83bb9db8065b3b7e9ef10a6aa751cf04f5c8cc) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - Saving or deleting a NAM model or a cabinet IR from the editor no longer reads the pedal's memory while the second processor may be working, which could stop that processor until the pedal was switched off.

- [#94](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/94) [`b83757e`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/b83757e10f14643cc0c7985f2c27b090aff01301) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The editor scales a NAM capture whose internal values would overflow the pedal's fixed point (such as CavLabs [#4](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/issues/4) - Gain 7 Boosted, which played as noise) so that it sounds the same within range, and its pedal panel credits the TONE3000 capture authors who gave permission for their captures to ship as factory amps.

- [#38](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/38) [`67b29c5`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/67b29c508e81c2f133eb495a8b49837befac0053) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - The editor no longer misses a change made on the pedal when the pedal answers one of its requests within 40 ms of the change.

- [#70](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/pull/70) [`6c70d1b`](https://github.com/VinSpee/undertone/commit/6c70d1b46e9f4bb3ca38121bbe00a4b1197c5499) Thanks [@VinSpee](https://github.com/VinSpee)! - MIDI clock sent over USB now sets the delay time (Gamut, with CLOCK > DELAY on) and PALETTE's tempo: the pedal now notes when each USB MIDI message arrives, which it never did before, so it could not time the clock.
