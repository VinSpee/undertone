The web editor connects to an M-VAVE Chocolate or Chocolate Plus over Bluetooth (in Chrome and Edge) and makes it drive the pedal: in the MIDI section it knows which model is connected and shows its controls, each footswitch is taught by pressing it, and you choose what it does: the next or the previous preset, one preset, an effect on or off, tap tempo for the delay, or a setting for the expression pedal. It works with the normal firmware while the editor is open, the mapping is kept in the browser for each model, and Undo takes a change back.
