Gamut gets ready for 48 kHz. A cabinet IR is now loaded at the pedal's own sample rate, and the pedal plays an IR made at the other rate resampled, so your saved IRs keep their sound when Gamut moves to 48 kHz; the editor's note on a NAM capture's sample rate now compares it with the pedal's. At 44.1 kHz the sound is unchanged. A firmware that runs at 48 kHz offers USB audio at 48 kHz only, and the simulated pedal's sound runs at 48 kHz.
