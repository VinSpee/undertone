Gamut's 12 factory amps are now NAM captures from TONE3000, shipped with their authors' permission: a Fender Super Reverb and a Vox AC30 by amalgamaudio, four channels of a Bogner TM 26 by torcuato, two Soldano SLO-100 by subterraneans, two Orange Dual Terror by chuggernautxanner and two Orange Jim Root Terror by CavLabs. The factory presets are rebuilt round them (the captures that hold their cabinet play with the CAB block off), and a preset you saved with a factory amp now plays the new model in the same place of the list.
