The pedal's start, crash and rescue screens are in Gamut's design. A start shows the mark, the name `gamut` in small letters and the version. The report of a start that failed is an amber band with a warning sign, what to do, and the figures for a bug report in two columns; the start after it says `safe mode` in amber. A crash is graphite on amber instead of white on red, and the USB rescue mode shows the mark with its lamp unlit. Nothing on the pedal's screen is red any more.
