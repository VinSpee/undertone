The pedal's screen has a new palette: a dark ground with pale text, on which a block that is on is a pale key and a block that is off is a dark key. Green is now a lamp and nothing else: it marks a block that is on, the playing preset in the list, a connected computer and a note in tune. Amber is the only warning colour, for an unsaved edit, a model that is loading, an overload, a late block, a low battery and a fault; nothing is drawn in red. The blocks no longer have colours of their own on the pedal, and the web editor keeps them.
