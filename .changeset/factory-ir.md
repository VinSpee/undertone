Slot IR1 now comes with a cabinet IR, "BEST IR IN THE WORLD" by ML Sound Lab ([video](https://www.facebook.com/watch/?ref=embed_video&v=1053603041469100)), redistributed with the creator's permission as stated by the project's owner: the factory presets play it until you load your own IR into IR1, and removing yours brings it back. The factory presets' levels are set for it.
