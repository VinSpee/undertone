The pedal's screen has new type: B612 for words and B612 Mono for figures, with labels, hints and settings in small letters (`gain`, `knob: block`, `363 ms`). The factory presets' names are in small letters too (`twin clean`, `user 25`); a sound you saved keeps the name it was saved with, and the names of models and IRs are shown as they are stored. The preset number and the tuner's note keep their old type for now.
